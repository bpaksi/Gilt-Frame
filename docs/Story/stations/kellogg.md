## The woman
**Name, dates:** Dr. Carrie Staines Kellogg (1867-1948) [2][3]
**Order title (fiction):** "The Physician." The one who kept watch over the Keeper's sight.
**Real fact(s) used in play:**
- A teacher before she became a physician; on the staff of the Battle Creek Sanitarium [3][4].
- Married W. K. Kellogg on 1 January 1918 [3][4]. The Manor House was their summer home; they crossed its threshold in October 1926 [2][5].
- In the Kelloggs' time a rose garden with a sundial and a bronze water bearer grew near the base of the stairway to the lake [5].
- Supporting figure (not the gallery woman): W. K. Kellogg, "The Keeper," who lost his sight to glaucoma late in life [1].
**Link type:** Order attribution. History records no tie between the Kelloggs and the 1893 Fair; the Order claims the garden was kept on purpose.

## Inbound clue
"There is a garden above a lake where the hours are kept in stone and four figures stand guard. A physician once walked there beside a man who was losing the light. Go and read what they left on the dial."

## Narrative
- **Base:** The dial has kept time here for a hundred years, and almost no one reads it. The woman who lived above this garden spent her life watching bodies for small signs others missed. We think she would have liked you. You read the dial.
- **Beat 1 Attention:** Most people walk past this garden on the way to the water. You stopped. The four of them have waited a long time for someone who would look at their faces instead of the view. We noticed that you did.
- **Beat 2 Courage:** She married a man in the years when his eyes were beginning to fail, and she knew what was coming. To stand beside someone as the light leaves them takes a steadiness most people never find. You stood here long enough to read what the dial keeps. That is the same kind of steadiness.
- **Beat 3 Risk:** The Keeper lost his sight in this house. She watched it go. To love someone who sees is to know that seeing can be taken. You looked hard at this place anyway. The Order has always asked that of its own.
- **Beat 4 Being seen:** A physician learns to see what a patient will not say. She watched quietly, for years, in rooms like the ones above you. You have been watched like that too, Sparrow. Kindly. For longer than you know.
- **Beat 5 Loss:** The roses she knew are gone. The dial stayed. Nothing in a garden keeps, and that is why people plant them. We bring you here to stand where something beautiful was, and to notice that it still is.
