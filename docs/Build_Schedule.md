## Risks, most dangerous first

1. **Twilio A2P approval (top risk).** The account is currently restricted and the game's whole magic runs on SMS/MMS. Resolution time is unpredictable and outside our control. This is why it starts in Phase 0 and gates Phase 4. If it slips past end of February, we should seriously consider holding launch or running a reduced first summer.
2. **Travel dates.** Phase 2 cannot finalize until you confirm the 2027 stops. Target: locked by end of November.
3. **Art rotation.** Paintings move. The Cassatt exhibition at the Art Institute is on now; I'll check whether the works we want are still up in summer 2027, and we build in fallbacks per location (the engine already supports this).
4. **Physical artifact lead times.** Cardstock, wax, custom seals, and printing all have tails. Order in Phase 3, not Phase 5.
5. **Personal hooks.** The ending only lands if the fragment-6 line is yours. Needed in Phase 1, or the emotional climax stays generic.
